What are Akan names?

Akan names originate from the Akan people of Ghana and parts of Côte d'Ivoire. Traditionally, children are given names based on the day of the week they are born.

These names are known as "day names" and are an important part of Akan identity and cultural heritage.

Why Does The Day Of Birth Matter?

In Akan culture, the day a child is born is believed to influence their name and represents a connection between the individual, their family, and their community.

More Than Just A Name

For the Akan people, names carry stories. They preserve family history, cultural values, and a person's connection to their community.

A name is not simply a way to identify someone; it represents belonging, heritage, and identity.

Akan Names Today

Today, Akan names continue to be used by many people around the world as a way of celebrating Ghanaian heritage and keeping cultural traditions alive.
